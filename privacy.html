1. Who I am

NotYourGrades is a browser extension made and sold by Sebastian Leon Diaz, an individual developer based in Florida, USA ("I", "me"). I am responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Contact: sebastian.08ld@gmail.com.

NotYourGrades is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Focus School Software or any school or school district.

2. What is stored on your device

Using Chrome's local extension storage, on your computer only:

  • whether editing is on, and whether the on-page edit counter is shown;
  • the what-if values you typed, so they survive a page refresh;
  • your licence state: whether you have paid, and the date your free trial started;
  • after you click Unlock or "I already paid", a random identifier issued by ExtensionPay (see section 4).

None of this is sent to me. Restore real grades in the popup erases the typed values; removing the extension erases everything.

3. What is never collected

Your grades, scores, name, student ID, school, teachers, classes, and assignment names are read from the page you are already viewing, used for arithmetic, and written back to that same page. They are never transmitted, never stored off your device, and never seen by me. I do not use analytics, telemetry, cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or advertising. I do not read your browsing history or any website other than the ones listed in section 11.

4. Buying the unlock

Until you click Unlock or "I already paid", the extension makes no network requests for payment or licensing. The 7-day trial is a date stored in your own browser and is never sent anywhere.

Payments are processed by ExtensionPay using Stripe. When you start a purchase or restore one:

  • ExtensionPay issues a random identifier and stores it in your browser. It is not linked to your grades or school.
  • The extension then periodically asks ExtensionPay one question: has this identifier paid? Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to ExtensionPay.
  • You enter your email address and payment card on ExtensionPay's and Stripe's own pages, never inside the extension. Stripe may also collect billing details (such as country and postal code), device and IP information for fraud prevention.

What I can see: that a purchase happened, its date and amount, and the email address used. What I cannot see: your card number or any grades. I use your email only to confirm your purchase, process refunds, and answer billing questions.

Legal basis (EU/UK): performance of a contract — you asked to buy something.

5. The contact form

The contact form sends only when you press Send. It is delivered to my email by Web3Forms. It sends:

  • your message and the category you picked;
  • your email address, only if you typed one (it is optional);
  • a screenshot, only if you attached one — resized in your browser first if large;
  • the address of the contact page and your browser's user-agent string, so bugs can be reproduced.

If sending fails, the page offers my email address instead; writing to it works like any email. I use messages only to reply and to fix problems. They are never added to a mailing list, used for marketing, or shared. Please don't include anyone's grades or name in a screenshot.

Legal basis (EU/UK): your consent, given by choosing to send the message.

7. How long data is kept

  • On-device data: until you restore your grades, clear extension storage, or uninstall.
  • Purchase records: for as long as needed to honour your purchase, plus any period required for tax and accounting records. ExtensionPay and Stripe keep their own records under their policies.
  • Contact messages: until the issue is resolved and no longer useful — and deleted sooner on request.

9. Students, children & schools

NotYourGrades is used by students, many of them under 18. That is why it is built to collect nothing on its own.

  • Children under 13 (COPPA). The extension does not collect personal information from anyone while it runs. The only ways personal information reaches me are buying the unlock and the contact form. Children under 13 should not use either without a parent or guardian, and must not enter their own email. If I learn I received personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, I delete it. Parents can contact me to review or delete anything about their child.
  • Teens and minors. Purchases by anyone under 18 should be made by, or with permission of, a parent or guardian.
  • Student records (FERPA and state student-privacy laws). I am not provided by, under contract with, or acting for any school. The extension never receives, transmits, or stores education records; your grades stay in your browser. I never use student information for advertising, build profiles of students, or sell or disclose student information.

10. Chrome Web Store disclosure

The use of information received from Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose — recalculating your own gradebook on your screen — and its licensing and support. It is not transferred for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or any unrelated purpose, and no human reads it except to answer a message you sent or a billing request.

11. Permissions

  • storage — saves the items in section 2 on your device.
  • Focus grade pages (*.focusschoolsoftware.com/focus/Modules.php*) — the only pages the extension's main code runs on. It reads the grades already displayed there.
  • ExtensionPay pages (extensionpay.com) — ExtensionPay's own bundled code runs only on its checkout and login pages so the extension learns when your payment completes. It reads nothing else.

The extension cannot read any other website you visit. No code is downloaded or run from remote servers.

12. Security

Grade data never leaves your browser, which is the strongest protection there is. Everything that is transmitted goes over encrypted HTTPS to the providers above. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach ever affected your information, I would notify you as required by law.
